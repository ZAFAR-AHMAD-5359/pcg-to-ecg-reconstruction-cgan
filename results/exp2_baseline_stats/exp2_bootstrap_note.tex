The Proposed model reaches a pooled Pearson correlation of 0.610 on the EPHNOGRAM test set. A naive segment-level bootstrap gives a narrow 95\% confidence interval of $[0.572,\,0.646]$. That interval is optimistic, because the 453 segments come from only three continuous recordings. A subject-clustered bootstrap that resamples the three recordings widens the interval to $[0.314,\,0.907]$, which honestly reflects the effective sample size.
